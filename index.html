Sustainable Agriculture

Kodgal Village, Telangana June 2026 - Ongoing

The Beginning

The AI Shastra began this initiative by cultivating agricultural land in Kodgal Village. The project was created to understand agriculture at the grassroots level and explore practical farming methods.

Our Approach

The land was prepared, quality seeds were selected, and regular monitoring was carried out throughout the cultivation process. The initiative focused on learning through real-world implementation.

Key Highlights

Prepared the land
Selected suitable crops
Began cultivation
Monitored crop growth

Outcome

The cultivation was successful, providing valuable insights into farming practices and laying the foundation for future agriculture-focused initiatives.
